Main goal:
Create a car rental website template for Moroccan rental agencies. The public website must attract customers from Google search, show available cars, allow customers to create an account, submit rental requests, and track their requests. The admin side must allow the agency owner to manage cars, requests, customers, agents, expenses, revenues, profits, and business performance.

Important payment model:
The application must use COD only.

COD means “Cash on Delivery / Paiement à l’agence”.

There is no online payment in the first version.

The customer submits a rental request online, then the agency calls the customer to confirm. If the customer confirms by phone, the customer has 12 hours to come physically to the agency and pay the required amount or deposit. If the customer does not come and pay within 12 hours, the request becomes abandoned and the selected car becomes available again.

Payment workflow:

1. Customer submits a rental request online.
2. Request status becomes PENDING.
3. Admin or agent receives the request.
4. Admin or agent calls the customer.
5. If the customer does not answer, status can become CALL_ATTEMPTED or UNDER_REVIEW.
6. If the customer confirms by phone, status becomes CALL_CONFIRMED.
7. When status becomes CALL_CONFIRMED, a 12-hour payment deadline starts.
8. During these 12 hours, the car is temporarily held.
9. The customer must come to the agency and pay physically.
10. If the customer comes and pays, admin or agent marks the payment as PAID_AT_AGENCY.
11. The request status becomes RESERVED.
12. The car status becomes RESERVED.
13. If the customer does not come within 12 hours, the request status becomes ABANDONED.
14. The car becomes AVAILABLE again.
15. If the rental period starts and the car is delivered, status becomes CAR_DELIVERED.
16. When the car is returned, status becomes CAR_RETURNED.
17. When the rental is closed, status becomes COMPLETED.

Important reservation logic:

* A car can receive a rental request while available.
* When a customer submits a request, the car should not be permanently reserved yet.
* After the admin or agent confirms the customer by phone, the car becomes temporarily held for 12 hours.
* During the 12-hour hold, the car should not be confirmed for another customer for the same date range.
* If the customer pays at the agency within 12 hours, the request becomes RESERVED.
* If the customer does not pay within 12 hours, the request becomes ABANDONED and the car becomes available again.
* Only RESERVED, CAR_DELIVERED, CAR_RETURNED, and COMPLETED rentals should block the car calendar.
* ABANDONED, CANCELLED, and REJECTED requests should not block availability.
* Only COMPLETED rentals should count as final revenue.
* RESERVED rentals can be shown as expected revenue, but not final revenue.

Rental request workflow:

1. Visitor opens the website.
2. Visitor searches for a car.
3. Visitor opens the car details page.
4. Visitor selects rental start date and return date.
5. System calculates estimated price.
6. Visitor creates a customer account or logs in.
7. Customer fills rental request form.
8. Customer submits request.
9. Request status becomes PENDING.
10. Admin or agent receives notification.
11. Admin or agent calls the customer.
12. If call is attempted, status becomes CALL_ATTEMPTED.
13. If customer confirms by phone, status becomes CALL_CONFIRMED and WAITING_AGENCY_PAYMENT.
14. System sets payment_deadline = current time + 12 hours.
15. Customer sees message: “Votre demande est confirmée par téléphone. Merci de passer à l’agence dans les 12 heures pour confirmer votre réservation par paiement.”
16. If customer comes to agency and pays, admin or agent marks payment as PAID_AT_AGENCY.
17. Request status becomes RESERVED.
18. Car status becomes RESERVED for selected dates.
19. If customer does not pay before deadline, system automatically marks request as ABANDONED.
20. Car becomes available again.
21. At rental start, admin or agent marks car as CAR_DELIVERED.
22. At return, admin or agent marks car as CAR_RETURNED.
23. After closing, status becomes COMPLETED.
24. Completed rental revenue is counted in BI dashboard.

Business logic:

* A car cannot be reserved twice for overlapping dates.
* Temporary holds expire after 12 hours if payment is not completed.
* If a car is in MAINTENANCE, it cannot be requested.
* If a request is confirmed by phone, create a temporary hold.
* If payment is completed at the agency, convert temporary hold to reserved block.
* If request is abandoned, release the temporary hold.
* If rental is completed, car becomes available again unless marked maintenance.
* Expenses must reduce profit.
* Only completed rentals count as final revenue.
* Reserved rentals count as expected revenue.
* Pending and waiting payment requests count as pipeline/pending revenue only.
* Abandoned, rejected, and cancelled requests do not count as revenue.
